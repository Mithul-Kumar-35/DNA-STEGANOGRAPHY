Conclusion

Steganography is powerful because it enables hidden channels. In modern cybersecurity it’s both a privacy tool and a threat vector—understanding it improves defensive monitoring, incident response, and secure design.

By combining steganography awareness with cryptography, network monitoring, and threat intelligence, defenders can better spot covert channels and protect critical data from exfiltration.
