Applications of Steganography

Steganography appears in both defensive and offensive cybersecurity scenarios. These examples highlight why defenders must understand hidden channels.

Secure communication

Covert messaging in high-surveillance environments, where even obvious encryption might be suspicious.

Digital watermarking

Invisible marks to prove ownership, detect tampering, or trace leaks of sensitive media.

Cyber warfare

Covert command-and-control and influence operations that blend in with normal multimedia traffic.

Data protection

Embedding identifiers, integrity checks, or recovery data in critical assets as a second line of defense.
