How Steganography Works

Steganography hides data inside a “cover” medium so that changes are visually or audibly subtle. Common carriers are images, audio, video, and even formatted text.

Image steganography

Commonly uses LSB (Least Significant Bit) changes in pixel channels to store bits with minimal visual impact.

Audio steganography

Embeds data in low-impact signal components (LSB, phase coding, spread spectrum) while preserving perceived sound.

Video steganography

Like image stego across many frames; can also use motion vectors or transform-domain methods.

Text steganography

Uses whitespace, punctuation patterns, synonym substitution, or formatting—typically lower capacity than media carriers.
