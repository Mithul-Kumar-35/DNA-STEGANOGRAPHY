Why Steganography Matters in Cybersecurity

In modern threat landscapes, steganography is a double-edged sword. Organizations use it for:

  • 🔐 Digital Watermarking: Protecting intellectual property and authentication
  • 🛡️ Covert Communication: Secure channels in high-threat environments
  • ⚠️ Threat Detection: Identifying unauthorized data exfiltration attempts
  • 📊 Steganalysis: Statistical analysis to detect hidden messages

Understanding steganography is essential for both defenders who need to detect covert channels and security professionals who implement privacy-preserving technologies.
